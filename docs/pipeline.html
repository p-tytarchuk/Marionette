Marionette: where we are

The dissertation is two papers. Paper 1: vector rigs from Lottie, and a 2.5D motion model (UniMate fine-tuned on UniML3D projections, then on Lottie curves). Paper 2: text to character and scene generation on a phone, built on Paper 1's data, motion model and assembly. The map follows the work plan step by step (1.1 to 1.10, 2.1 to 2.8; the plan's own drawing is at the bottom of this page); each box says what exists in this folder today. Click a box for its manual, which starts with the plan's wording for that step.
