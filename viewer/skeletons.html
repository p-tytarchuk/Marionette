Role colours exist where the roles are known: every synthetic skeleton (by construction) and the Anymate predicted rigs (roles from anymate_roles.py). The other real skeletons have no role labels; their class is the dataset's own label (the manifest category, AnyTop's group, Qwen's body plan for Lottie rigs). Limbs on the far side of the body are drawn fainter. Lottie rigs are drawn in 2D already, so the yaw does not change them.
